## Key Learnings
- **Horizon-freeze root cause**: the event-driven engine samples continuous event times → no event lands exactly on the horizon → the advance loop froze `now` just below it while `_is_done` required `>=` → every episode silently ran to `max_episode_steps` (20k) of no-op steps, each paying a full policy forward. Both serval v3 "stalls" (16 CPU-h) and the local 0-eps repro were this. Under dt-polling it was masked (dt-aligned events hit integer horizons exactly).
- **The 5M collapse is an objective problem, NOT distribution shift**: fine-tuning hc_v3_last for 60 eps on horizons U(1.5M, 5M) (matched T2V scale, halved LR) left 5M performance flat (132.0k vs 132.4k) with the illness blowup unchanged (73k vs heuristics' ~46k) — after *training on* deep-horizon states. γ-myopia + unpriced illness is the mechanism. Bonus: the FT improved baseline scale to 3050 (best learned agent there).
- **Knowledge-churn exploit**: under decay, the floored `knowledge_increment = max(0, Δ)` pays for letting knowledge drop (unpenalized) and re-learning it (paid again) — churn earns more than maintenance. Un-floored PBRS prices losses and makes maintenance valuable. Live probe proof: 384/600 random-policy steps had negative increments post-flip (impossible pre-flip).
- **PBRS invariance under semi-MDP discounting** requires the shaping discount to match the agent's per-transition discount γ^Δt; a fixed γₚ leaks a spurious reward correlated with decision spacing (over-values future potential on long gaps).
- **Per-decision discounting is anti-time-consistent**: the busier the factory, the more myopic the agent in *time units* — exactly when consequences matter most; also makes fixed γ mean different time horizons at different scales.
- **The v4 world reshapes the leaderboard**: decay breaks knowledge saturation (5M fleet knowledge 1.4M → ~75k maintained quantity, killing the reviewer's "saturation artifact" critique); everyone loses ~11% industrial throughput; hc_v3_last climbs to #6 industrial (2268), passing SPT† (2126) and ReserveSpec† — a harsher world favors the knowledge-aware policy. But the v3 family's 5M knowledge (57–59k) is *below* top heuristics (70–76k): its assignment style fails to maintain knowledge under decay.
- **v3-generation headline (old world)**: hc_v3 best learned agent at industrial (2561, #1 fleet knowledge 87k, fewest illness 957, −3.4% vs reward oracle); informed baselines still top raw throughput; anchors (PPO-TRF 12/22, Rainbow 20/22) can't transfer scales — the §7.4 story.
- **γ=1 in PPO breaks the critic** (value target = whole remaining episode over 10⁴–10⁶ steps; PopArt fixes scale, not variance; variable horizons make returns differ ~75% by construction).
